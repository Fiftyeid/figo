Thirteen categories. Forty-four aircraft. This is the register of everything the United States Navy and Marine Corps puts in the air in 2026, from a primary trainer that first flew in the fifties to a combat-coded F-35 off a carrier deck. Airframes retire, squadrons re-equip, and the list is rewritten every year. But the shape of it stays the same: a fleet built to launch from a deck, land on a deck, and hold the line from the sea. That is the 2026 register.
