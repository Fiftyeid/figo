Sources and years:
- 2024 USAF & USSF Almanac, Air & Space Forces Magazine, June 7, 2024. Air Force total inventory 5,286 as of Sept 30, 2023, including unmanned aircraft and targets. Manned figure 4,959 (derived: 5,286 minus 327 unmanned), including 25 gliders. https://www.airandspaceforces.com/article/2024-usaf-ussf-almanac-equipment/
- Congressional Budget Office, "Availability and Use of Aircraft in the Army," Sept 2024. About 3,900 manned Army aircraft (2023). About 700 large unmanned aerial systems. Smaller tactical drones are not included. https://www.cbo.gov/publication/60313 and https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2024-08/60313-army-aircraft.pdf
- Congressional Research Service, In Focus IF12803 (Nov 1, 2024). Navy and Marine Corps reported together, about 4,000 aircraft, FY2022, citing the DoD Annual Aviation Inventory and Funding Plan. https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/IF/HTML/IF12803.html
- FlightGlobal, 2026 World Air Forces Directory (public summary): just over 52,200 aircraft, 161 nations. Scale check only, not a ranking. https://www.flightglobal.com/defence/2025/11/2026-world-air-forces-directory/

All figures are rounded where CBO says "about". Figures are not ranked across different years or definitions.

Not used in this video: USMC 1,262 (2022 plan, different year); USAF FY2025 total 4,865 (category definition changed); Navy budget subset figures; aggregator websites.
