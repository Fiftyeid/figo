How many US military aircraft are there? It depends on what you count, and which branch you ask about.
This video compares US military aircraft counts for the Air Force, Army, and Navy with Marine Corps, with the year and source shown for each figure.

The appearance of U.S. Department of War (DoW) visual information does not imply or constitute DoW endorsement.

This video is an analytical comparison based on publicly available information.

Sources and years:
- Air Force: 2024 USAF & USSF Almanac, Air & Space Forces Magazine (June 7, 2024). Total inventory 5,286 as of Sept 30, 2023, including unmanned aircraft and targets. Manned figure 4,959 is derived (5,286 minus 327 unmanned), and includes 25 gliders. https://www.airandspaceforces.com/article/2024-usaf-ussf-almanac-equipment/
- Army: Congressional Budget Office, "Availability and Use of Aircraft in the Army" (August 2024; publication page Sept 2024). About 3,900 manned Army aircraft in 2023, and about 700 large unmanned aerial systems. Smaller tactical drones are not included. https://www.cbo.gov/publication/60313 and https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2024-08/60313-army-aircraft.pdf
- Navy and Marine Corps: Congressional Research Service, In Focus IF12803 (Nov 1, 2024). The source reports the Navy and Marine Corps together, about 4,000 aircraft, FY2022. Army mission statement also from this source. https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/IF/HTML/IF12803.html
- Scale check: FlightGlobal, 2026 World Air Forces Directory (public summary). Just over 52,200 aircraft in 161 nations. https://www.flightglobal.com/defence/2025/11/2026-world-air-forces-directory/

Figures are rounded where the source says "about." Counts from different years or definitions are not ranked against each other. Not used in this video: USMC-only 1,262 (2022); Navy budget subset figures; country figures from paywalled sources.
