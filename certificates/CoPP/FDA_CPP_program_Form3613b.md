# Getting a CPP from the US FDA — program & application requirements

> **Source (public FDA presentation, CDER Office of Compliance — Imports Exports Compliance Branch):**
> <https://www.fda.gov/media/91749/download>
> FDA CPP information page: <https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-exports/certificate-pharmaceutical-product-cpp>
> Example completed Form 3613b: see “Links to Examples of Completed Form 3613b” on the FDA CPP page.

## What is a CPP? (FDA, CPG 7150.01)

- Certificate to export **human drug products (including biological drugs)**.
- **Conforms to the WHO certification requirements.**
- Contains information about the pharmaceutical regulatory / marketing status of the product in the U.S.

## Certificates may be issued for

1. Approved drug product
2. Over-the-counter (OTC) drug product
3. Unapproved drug product
4. Homeopathic drug
5. Drug in a bulk package (e.g., API)

## Who can apply?

Anyone who exports a drug may submit a complete application for export certification.
Certification is intended for a drug which meets the applicable requirements of the
Federal Food, Drug and Cosmetic Act (801(e)(1) / 21 U.S.C. 381(e)(1)).

## Process to apply

- Submit **Form FDA 3613b** (FDA is transitioning to accept CPP applications solely via Form 3613b).
- Electronic applications: **CDER Export Certification Application and Tracking System (CDER eCATS)**
  — eCPPs are issued as downloadable PDFs. An online Account ID (FURLS) is needed.
- Biologicals: Biologics Export Certification application and Tracking system.

## Required CPP application information

| Category | Items |
|---|---|
| Applicant | Name of applicant · Applicant contact information · Status of applicant · Authorization to release information · Billing contact · Federal Tax Identification Number (TIN) |
| Product | U.S. trade name (brand name) · Bulk substance generic name · Number of certificates requested |
| Status | FDA marketing authority (NDA/ANDA/AADA/BLA or monograph) · Marketing status in the exporting country (U.S.) · Status of product licence holder |
| Manufacturing | Complete manufacturing facility address · Facility registration number · Listing of manufacturing location on CPP |
| Destination | Country of destination (importing country) |

## Additional required information (per product type)

**Approved drug products:** NDA/ANDA/AADA approval letter · Container labels · Immediate package container · Package insert · Status of product licence holder.

**OTC drug products:** Title of the applicable monograph · Product labels · Immediate package container label.

**Unapproved drug products:** (details in FDA guidance — statement that the drug meets 801(e)(1) requirements, etc.)

## Useful notes

- Certificate expires **2 years from the notarization date** or as noted; after expiry a new
  application must be submitted.
- Export notification is **not required** for approved drugs and drugs distributed in compliance
  with the Act; required for certain unapproved drugs per FDCA 802(g).
- Online verification of eCPPs: FDA Export Certificate Validator (FECV).
- Exporter record-keeping requirements (802(b)): trade name, strength/dosage form, importing
  country, lot/control number, consignee name & address, export date, quantity, and statements
  that the product meets the foreign purchaser’s specifications and does not conflict with the
  laws of the importing country.

## Other countries issuing CPPs (WHO-format)

| Country | Authority |
|---|---|
| USA | FDA (CDER eCATS) |
| EU member states | National competent authorities (HMA network) |
| UK | MHRA |
| Switzerland | Swissmedic |
| India | CDSCO (COPP issued by zonal offices on behalf of DCG(I)) |
| Egypt | EDA — Egyptian Drug Authority |
| Jordan | JFDA |
| Saudi Arabia | SFDA |

> In every case the CPP is issued **by the regulatory authority of the exporting country**, not by
> the manufacturer. If you are importing/registering a product, you request the CPP **from your
> supplier (the MAH)**, who applies to its own national authority.
