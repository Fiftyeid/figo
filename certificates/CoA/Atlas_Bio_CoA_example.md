> **Source (public PDF from the company website):**
> <https://www.atlasbio.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Atlas-Bio-COA-example.pdf>
> Product: **Equalfetal® Bovine Serum** (100% Bovine Neonatal Serum) — example of a real
> Certificate of Analysis issued by a US biologicals manufacturer.
> This is a public sample published by the company itself for illustration of their CoA format.

This example shows how a real manufacturer CoA is structured in practice — note how it maps to
the WHO model (product ID block → test table with Specification / Results / Method → QA release block).

## What to note (vs. the WHO model)

- The company header + catalog/lot/product block = WHO “product identification” fields.
- Each test line carries **Specification → Results → Method reference**, exactly like the WHO model table.
- Methods reference **pharmacopoeias (USP/EP) and regulations (9 CFR, 21 CFR)** — the “method reference” column of the WHO model.
- Ends with a QA signature/date/issue-number release block = WHO “conclusion & approval” section.
