## Synthesis: Subject Comparison Summary

| Subject | Best BSR Found | Est. Sales/Mo | Competition (Reviews) | Entry Difficulty | Trend |
|---------|---------------|--------------|----------------------|-----------------|-------|
| Flowers | ~82,813 | ~90 | HIGH (500+, RED) | Hard | Mature/stable |
| Cozy Rooms | ~313,056 | ~12 | LOW (<100, GREEN) | Easy | Rising |
| Ocean | ~343,713 | ~12 | LOW (<100, GREEN) | Easy | Stable |
| Cats | ~809,438 | ~1–2 | MODERATE (new crowding) | Moderate | Crowding |
| Birds | ~587,897 | ~1–2 | LOW (<100, GREEN) | Easy | Moderate |
| Butterflies | n/a (data gap) | unknown | LOW (data limited) | Likely Easy | Stable |
| Turtles | n/a (data gap) | <1 (inferred) | VERY LOW (<10) | Very Easy | Niche |

**WINNER: Bold and Easy Cozy Rooms / Cottagecore**

Rationale: The best BSR among the low-competition subjects (~313,056 = 12 sales/mo), apparent near-zero review counts on top competing titles, alignment with the November 2026 gifting season (hygge/cozy aesthetic peaks in autumn/winter), and a rising rather than declining trend all point to cozy rooms/cottagecore as the single most profitable subject to enter now.

**Runner-up: Ocean / Sea Life**

Ocean is virtually tied on demand (BSR ~343,713) with similar low competition. The added appeal of a sea turtle element (a popular stand-alone subject) could differentiate a title.

**Notable: If willing to pursue a flowers-adjacent combination,** a "Bold & Easy Flowers + Butterflies" or "Bold & Easy Birds + Flowers" combined title captures the high-demand flowers keyword while targeting a less saturated angle, as demonstrated by the #63,765 BSR seen on a combined birds+flowers title.

**Avoid: Cats and pure Flowers** — cats are becoming crowded in the bold-and-easy style with many 2025 releases, and pure flowers face entrenched competition from established publishers.
