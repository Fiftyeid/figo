## Q1: What exact keywords and search phrases do buyers use on Amazon for each niche? (long-tail vs broad)

### Takeaway
Each niche has a distinct vocabulary layered across broad, mid-tail, and long-tail tiers. Broad terms drive volume but face overwhelming publisher competition; long-tail phrases with intent modifiers (audience, mood, use-case) are where new KDP entrants can realistically rank.

### Takeaway

- "cottagecore coloring book": under 200 direct competing books estimated for the exact phrase (KDPEasy vendor blog; unverified). — [KDPEasy Best Coloring Book Niches](https://www.kdpeasy.com/blog/best-coloring-book-niches-kdp)
- Gothic dark fantasy competition: broad keywords ("coloring book", "adult coloring") put sellers in direct competition with large publishers; niche-specific angles recommended. Dark fairy tales case study warns that broad keywords lead to competition with massive publishing houses. — [Dark Fairy Tales Coloring Book case](https://ava.ead.domalberto.edu.br/dark-fairy-tales-coloring-book)
- "dark academia" adjacent sub-niche: fewer than 50 books cited (one vendor blog; unverified, check directly on Amazon). — [KDPEasy FAQ coloring books](https://www.kdpeasy.com/faq/coloring-books)
- Mushroom coloring book: ~891 Amazon search results for the exact phrase (seller claim, undated, unverified). — [mushroom KDP niche discussion](https://brand-development-haven.pages.dev/mushroom-coloring-pages-for-kdp-what-most-sellers-get-wrong-and-how/)
- Review count thresholds used by KDP community: <100 reviews on top books = easy to compete; <500 = manageable; >2,000 = entrenched. — [KDP Opportunity Score discussion](https://univers.studio/keyword-research/); [KDP niche navigator Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=12081)
- Specialist dark fantasy coloring books have review counts in the range of 2–623 based on third-party "best of" roundup pages (unreliable; not verified against Amazon directly). — [asinsight.com adult coloring market](https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/adult-coloring-books)

## Q5: What scoring methodology do KDP experts use to evaluate niche opportunity?

### Takeaway
KDP experts use a Demand vs. Competition matrix — primarily BSR and review count — to generate an Opportunity Score. No single universal standard exists; the most commonly cited formula is a simple ratio, while tool-specific scores (Publisher Rocket, Univers Studio, Titans Pro) add weighted inputs. All are heuristics, not Amazon-verified metrics.

### Cited Findings

**Core Formula (most cited by community blogs)**
- Opportunity Score = (Estimated Monthly Searches ÷ 1,000) × (1 ÷ Number of Competing Books)
- This formula produces higher scores for niches with more demand relative to supply. — [KDP Opportunity Score discussion, Univers Studio](https://univers.studio/keyword-research/)

**KDPEasy's 0–25 Rubric**
- Score 0–25 on a green/yellow/red scale.
- Demand signal: number of top-10 results with BSR under 100,000 (more = stronger demand).
- Competition signal: average review count of top 5 listings; higher reviews = tougher competition.
- Green zone = strong demand + low review counts; red zone = either low demand or many high-review competitors. — [KDPEasy KDP Niche Research System](https://www.kdpeasy.com/blog/kdp-niche-research-system)

**Univers Studio Niche Scorer (0–10)**
- Inputs: BSR, review count, price, and publication age of top 10 results.
- Score >7 = excellent opportunity (high demand, low competition).
- Score 5–7 = good potential, more competition.
- Score <5 = too competitive or insufficient demand. — [Univers Studio Keyword Research](https://univers.studio/keyword-research/)

**Publisher Rocket's Output (tool, not open formula)**
- Reports: estimated monthly searches, number of competing books, average price, estimated monthly earnings, and competitive score.
- Exact internal scoring formula is not public. — [Publisher Rocket Review, Low Content Profits](https://lowcontentprofits.com/publisher-rocket-review-kdp/)

**Titans Pro Demand/Opportunity Score**
- Returns demand score (e.g., 6/10) and opportunity score (e.g., 9/10) per keyword.
- Methodology is proprietary; outputs include Amazon search volume, number of results, demand score, and opportunity score. — [Titans Pro KDP Tutorial, Low Content Profits](https://lowcontentprofits.com/titans-pro-amazon-kdp-keyword-research/)

**Community-Sourced Thresholds**
- Review thresholds for assessing competition level:
  - <100 reviews on top books = easy to enter
  - 100–500 reviews = moderate competition
  - 500–2,000 reviews = competitive, requires differentiation
  - >2,000 reviews = entrenched market, very hard for new entrant — [Vappingo KDP Competition Analyzer](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=12097); [Univers Studio](https://univers.studio/keyword-research/)
- BSR benchmark: a BSR under 100,000 on the top 3–5 results indicates active, ongoing sales. — [KDPEasy Niche Research System](https://www.kdpeasy.com/blog/kdp-niche-research-system)
- Quality caveat: 500 poorly made competitors < 100 high-quality competitors in terms of opportunity. — [Vappingo KDP Competition Analyzer](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=12097)

### Inferences
- A practical scoring approach for a new entrant without paid tools: search the exact keyword on Amazon, count the results, check BSR and review count on the top 5 listings, and apply the community formula. If the top 5 average <200 reviews and BSR <80,000, the niche is worth entering.
- Scoring tools diverge on weighting, so using two different tools and comparing outputs is a commonly suggested validation step.

### Gaps
- Publisher Rocket's exact formula is proprietary and not publicly documented.
- No side-by-side comparison of tool scores for the three specific niches was found.

## Q6: Based on keyword data, which niche scores highest for a new entrant?

### Takeaway
Cottagecore / Cozy Cottage appears to offer the best opportunity for a new entrant based on available evidence: fewest direct competitors, clearly trending consumer aesthetic, and confirmed retail publisher investment (a signal of genuine buyer demand without yet saturating the KDP market. Mushroom/Forest rates a close second; Gothic/Dark Fantasy is highest-volume but most competitive.

### Cited Findings

**Comparative Competition Data**
- Cottagecore: "under 200 books" for direct phrase (vendor claim, unverified). — [KDPEasy Best Coloring Niches](https://www.kdpeasy.com/blog/best-coloring-book-niches-kdp)
- Mushroom: ~891 Amazon results for "mushroom coloring book" (seller marketing claim, undated, unverified). — [mushroom KDP niche discussion](https://brand-development-haven.pages.dev/mushroom-coloring-pages-for-kdp-what-most-sellers-get-wrong-and-how/)
- Gothic dark fantasy (broad): over 200,000 coloring book listings on Amazon in total; this niche competes with established publishers (Quarto, Creative Haven). — [Bookbolt coloring books](https://bookbolt.io/?p=4694)

**Trend Signals**
- Cottagecore aesthetic: active retail publisher investment in 2025 (Quarto titles); identified as "currently trending" with low SKU count. — [Quarto Cottagecore Coloring](https://quarto.com/books/9780785846505/cottagecore-coloring-book)
- Mushroom/nature: "durable" trend, described as holding multi-year interest; scientifically accurate mushroom illustration specifically cited as a rare, differentiated angle. — [KDP niche guide blog](https://brand-development-haven.pages.dev/mushroom-coloring-pages-for-kdp-what-most-sellers-get-wrong-and-how/)
- Gothic/dark fantasy: declining trend signal for "gothic coloring" (-17% YoY per RankHero); longer-established niche. — [RankHero gothic coloring](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/gothic-coloring)

**Profitability Benchmarks**
- Niche mandala coloring books (specialist): ~$1,200/month earnings estimate vs ~$200/month for generic books (vendor statistic; no methodology shown). — [KDPEasy](https://www.kdpeasy.com/blog/best-coloring-book-niches-kdp)

**Scoring Summary (applied using community formula with best available estimates)**

| Niche | Est. Monthly Searches (keyword) | Direct Competitors | Opportunity Score (formula) | Trend | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cottagecore / Cozy Cottage | Unknown (trending, medium est.) | <200 (unverified) | High (denominator small) | Rising | Best for new entrant |
