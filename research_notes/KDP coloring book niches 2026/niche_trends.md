# Amazon KDP Coloring Book Niches 2026 — Niche Trends Research

## Key Question 1: What coloring book themes are currently trending on Amazon and social media (2025-2026)?

### Takeaway
"Cozy coloring" (thick-line, low-pressure, hygge-aesthetic designs) has become the dominant TikTok-driven trend in 2025-2026, alongside a renewed mainstream surge in adult coloring driven by anxiety and stress-relief demand, with the Bold and Easy aesthetic capturing an estimated 40% of best-selling titles.

### Cited Findings
- The "Bold and Easy" aesthetic — simple thick-line designs optimized for relaxation — reportedly captures approximately 40% of best-selling coloring book titles on Amazon as of 2026 — [KDP Easy Coloring Book Trends 2026](https://www.kdpeasy.com/guides/coloring-book-trends-2026)
- "Cozy coloring" is a TikTok-driven style defined by thick outlines, soft themes, and low-pressure content; it dominates what users call "ColorTok" and is linked to slow, low-pressure evenings — [AOL/Rise of Cozy Coloring article, 2026](https://www.aol.com/articles/rise-cozy-coloring-finding-calm-090526323.html) (page unreachable for direct fetch; source surfaced in search)
- Adult coloring books are described as "bestsellers again" in a March 2026 analysis, with the driving force being widespread anxiety and a need for unplugged calm — [Daily Maverick, March 11, 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-03-11-adult-colouring-books-are-bestsellers-again-it-makes-sense-in-our-anxious-age.md) (page unreachable for direct fetch; source surfaced in search)
- US children's coloring and activity print books sold 16.7 million units in 2025, up +18% year-over-year — this was the strongest growth segment in children's print (vs. +3% for children's print overall) — [coloringbook.dev citing Circana/Publishers Weekly data](https://coloringbook.dev/blog/best-selling-coloring-book-niches-2026) (page unreachable; claim surfaced in search result snippet)
- Grayscale photo coloring is called "the fastest-growing adult coloring niche in 2026" in one KDP guide — [KDP Easy Coloring Book Trends 2026](https://www.kdpeasy.com/guides/coloring-book-trends-2026)
- Nostalgia/retro themes (vintage advertising, 1950s diners, Art Deco patterns) are trending with millennial and Gen X buyers — [Accio Business Trends 2025](https://www.accio.com/business/popular-coloring-book-niches-trend-2025)
- TikTok search interest for "tiktok coloring book" peaked at 69 in November 2025 on Accio's tracking — [Accio TikTok Coloring Trend](https://www.accio.com/business/tiktok-coloring-pages-trend) (note: Accio figures are unverified against Google Trends; treat as directional)
- Mandala and nature coloring books peaked in Amazon search volume at 1,080 monthly searches in December 2025, suggesting seasonal demand (holiday gifting) — [Accio Amazon KDP Coloring Book Trends 2025](https://www.accio.com/business/amazon-kdp-coloring-book-trends-2025)

### Inferences
- The "cozy/hygge" aesthetic and anxiety-relief positioning are complementary angles for the same product; titles that combine both in cover copy likely outperform generic "adult coloring" books.
- TikTok flip-through videos are an effective marketing channel, which means books with visually distinctive, high-contrast pages will perform better in short-form video marketing.
- The 18% growth in children's coloring activity print signals that kids' coloring books are a structurally stronger category than the adult segment right now.

### Gaps
- No verified sales rank or unit data for grayscale coloring books specifically; direct Amazon Best Sellers check required.
- Could not confirm AOL "cozy coloring" article data with a direct fetch due to network restrictions; treat as contextual.

## Key Question 2: Which niches show high search volume but relatively few quality listings on Amazon?

### Takeaway
Adult color-by-number books have confirmed high search volume (~20,000 Amazon searches/week) but are dominated by a few top players, while profession-specific, breed-specific, and stacked-niche books (e.g., "bold easy mandala seniors") show high demand-to-competition ratios based on BSR and review data from independent analyses.

### Cited Findings
- Amazon US weekly search volume for "color by numbers for adults" was approximately 20,229 searches during July 5–11, 2026, per ASINSIGHT third-party tracking — [ASINSight Color by Numbers Adults Report](https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/color-by-numbers-for-adults)
- Top 3 products for the "color by numbers for adults" keyword capture 43.7% of click share and 24.4% of conversion share, suggesting dominant incumbents but room below — [ASINSight Adult Color By Number Report](https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/adult-color-by-number)
- An analysis of 500 Amazon KDP coloring books found the following average Best Sellers Ranks: anxiety/mindfulness books (47,300 BSR), profession-specific books (52,100 BSR), breed-specific pet books (58,700 BSR) vs. generic animal books at ~312,000 BSR — [inkchamps.com 10 Best Niches 2026](https://inkchamps.com/blog/best-niches-for-coloring-books-amazon-kdp-2026/) (site unreachable for direct fetch; data cited in search snippets)
- A broad mandala book faces 50,000+ competitors on Amazon; a niche-mandala book with a zodiac theme reportedly has ~200 competitors, based on KDP Easy research — [KDP Easy 15+ Best Coloring Book Niches](https://www.kdpeasy.com/blog/best-coloring-book-niches-kdp)
- Breed-specific pet books averaged 98 reviews at a BSR of ~58,700 vs. broad animal books averaging 19 reviews at a BSR of ~312,000 — [inkchamps.com analysis](https://inkchamps.com/blog/best-niches-for-coloring-books-amazon-kdp-2026/) (page unreachable; cited in search)
- An axolotl niche was scored at 57 (low competition) and capybara at 66 (slightly more competition) in one third-party niche-scoring tool — [inkfluenceai.com Coloring Book Niches 2026](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/coloring-book-niches-2026)
- KDP search result count for "mushroom coloring book" was 891 results at a time when monthly Google search volume for the term was 480 — suggesting moderate demand, light competition — [recapio.com/lowcontentprofits.com KDP niches analysis](https://recapio.com/digest/kdp-niches-that-survived-the-saturation-wave-by-jeffsetlife) (note: data vintage uncertain, may predate 2025)
- "Jesus coloring book" shows monthly Amazon searches of roughly 390–720 from September 2025 to January 2026 (peaking in November-December), with a keyword difficulty of 37/100 (moderate) — [RankHero keyword data](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/jesus-coloring-book)

### Inferences
- Color-by-number for adults is a proven search demand but requires a distinguishing angle (theme, price, page count) to break through incumbent leaders.
- Stacked niches like "Bold Easy Large Print Mandala Coloring Book for Seniors" are described by multiple sources as the clearest low-competition, high-demand opening in 2026 — combining two or more proven demand segments in one title.
- Faith-based coloring has reliable if modest volume and a seasonal Q4 spike; ideal as a secondary niche rather than a primary business.

### Gaps
- Mushroom coloring book search data is from an older analysis; current 2026 competitor count not confirmed.
- No independent verification of the 500-book analysis BSR figures; inkchamps.com could not be fetched directly due to network restrictions.

## Key Question 3: What sub-niches of popular categories (animals, fantasy, mindfulness, etc.) are underserved?

### Takeaway
Within popular parent categories, specific-animal sub-niches (axolotl, capybara, highland cow), cultural heritage designs (Celtic knots, Japanese gardens), and profession-specific mindfulness books (nurse, teacher stress relief) are most consistently cited as underserved with meaningful demand.

### Cited Findings
- KDPBuilder (July 2026) names "grumpy cats, capybaras, highland cows, axolotls" as the growth sub-niches within animal coloring books, saying a "bold and easy capybara coloring book for adults" will outsell a "beautiful animal coloring book" significantly — [KDPBuilder Best Coloring Book Niches](https://kdpbuilder.com/blog/best-coloring-book-niches)
- An analysis of 500 KDP coloring books found Celtic knot and Japanese garden themes show low competition; the article reports these underperformed in raw sales but showed strong margins due to low competition — [inkchamps.com 10 Best Niches 2026](https://inkchamps.com/blog/best-niches-for-coloring-books-amazon-kdp-2026/)
- Hachette published "Super-Cute Axolotls: A Cozy Creatures Coloring Book" in January 2026, and a companion axolotl title in September 2025, demonstrating that traditional publishers are already validating this sub-niche — [Hachette Book Group listing](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/mafalda-mota/super-cute-axolotls/9798894143040)
- "The Biggest Puppies, Capybaras, Axolotls, Oh My! Cute Coloring Book" (Chartwell, June 2026, 256 pages) shows major-publisher investment in combined cute-animal coloring — [BulkBookStore listing](https://bulkbookstore.com/the-biggest-puppies-capybaras-axolotls-oh-my-ultimate-cute-coloring-book-9780785849308)
- Profession gift books (nurses, teachers) are described as having low competition per profession and strong gift-purchase demand — [KDP Easy Best Coloring Book Niches](https://www.kdpeasy.com/blog/best-coloring-book-niches-kdp)
- Dot-marker books for toddlers (hollow large circles for use with dot markers rather than pencils) are an underserved kids sub-niche with dedicated retail demand — [KDP Low Content Profits analysis](https://lowcontentprofits.com/low-content-book-niches-kdp/)
- Specific-age-band kids books (e.g., "Dinosaur Coloring Book for Boys Ages 3-8") perform better than generic children's titles due to Amazon's category algorithm favoring age-specified listings — [BookCoversLab KDP Niches 2026](https://bookcoverslab.com/blog/best-kdp-niches-2026)
- Unicorn and fantasy books for girls ages 3-10 are "evergreen" with top sellers routinely in the top-5,000 BSR — [crayonsparks.com Best KDP Niches 2026](https://www.crayonsparks.com/blog/best-kdp-niches-2026)

### Inferences
- Traditional publishers (Hachette, Chartwell) validating axolotl/capybara sub-niches confirms demand but also signals increasing competition; solo publishers should move quickly or target narrower angles (e.g., single-animal, specific age range).
- The pattern of genre-specific books with profession gifting (nurses, teachers, librarians) is replicable across dozens of professions — most are still underpublished.
- Dot-marker books are a genuinely format-differentiated sub-niche that requires a physical production consideration (larger hollow circles); this creates a small barrier that filters out low-effort competitors.

### Gaps
- No confirmed data on highland cow coloring books specifically; mentioned in multiple guides but no independent sales evidence found.
- Witchcraft/cottagecore coloring books: searched for but found no specific KDP niche data or verified demand signals in 2025-2026.

## Key Question 4: Are there any emerging cultural, lifestyle, or hobby trends creating new coloring book demand?

### Takeaway
Wellness culture and "dopamine dressing" aesthetics (bold, saturated designs) are driving demand for mindfulness and cozy-aesthetic coloring. Mushroom/foraging aesthetics and nostalgia/retro themes are emerging hobby-tied niches. Viral TikTok-tied designs represent a new but risky category requiring rapid publishing.

### Cited Findings
- Gothic florals, botanical slow-living scenes, and hobby-specific themes like gardening-and-cats are described as easier-to-market niches in a coloring book trends blog — [coloring market trends blog 2026](https://cbe-web.pages.dev/blog/adult-coloring-books-market-size-trends-2025-2026)
- Some books are built around trending TikTok sounds, meme formats, and creator personas; publishers monitor the "For You" page to track trending sounds, hashtags, and visual styles — [Accio TikTok Coloring Trend article](https://www.accio.com/business/tiktok-coloring-pages-trend)
- Nostalgia and retro aesthetics (vintage advertising, 1950s diners, Art Deco patterns) are trending with millennial and Gen X buyers — [Accio Popular Coloring Book Niches 2025](https://www.accio.com/business/popular-coloring-book-niches-trend-2025)
- Mushroom coloring books have moderate demand on Amazon KDP and "not huge yet" competition, per one KDP seller's self-published research — [Gumroad/mushroom coloring seller](https://subhamalik.gumroad.com/l/mushroom-coloring-book-vol-1) (seller's own product, treat as anecdotal)
- The "2026 Coloring Book Trends Nobody Expected to Explode" article (bottegadelsarto.com) lists nostalgia themes as a headline trend; however this site is of questionable credibility and uses unsourced "Johns Hopkins trial" citations — [shop.bottegadelsarto.com](https://shop.bottegadelsarto.com/opinion/2026-coloring-book-trends-749289) — treat with caution
- A Substack newsletter covering KDP through 2026 describes mushroom, cottagecore, and "slow life" aesthetics (kitchen gardens, cottage interiors) as growing micro-niches — [Naomi Jane Substack KDP 2026](https://naomijane.substack.com/p/low-and-medium-content-books-on-amazon)
- A 2026 trends article reports a 32% rise in Amazon keyword searches for cozy themes like "kitchen gardens," though the source gives no methodology — [bookcoverslab.com Low-Competition KDP Niches 2026](https://bookcoverslab.com/blog/low-competition-kdp-niches-2026) (cite with caution)
- Eco and nature themes plus cross-franchise fantasy scenes top trends in a kids-focused community forum for 2025 — [LucasGift Forum 2025 Kids Coloring Trends](https://lucasgift.com/community/lucasgift-forums/topic/155316/what-are-the-top-trending-coloring-pages-kids-in-2025)

### Inferences
- Mushroom/foraging and cottagecore aesthetics are still early-stage niches on KDP with low product counts; a publisher moving in 2026 may still find meaningful low-competition windows.
- TikTok-tied coloring books face a timing risk: by the time a print-on-demand book is live, the trend may have faded. Better to target evergreen lifestyle aesthetics (cozy, botanical, slow life) that share visual language with TikTok trends but aren't dependent on any single viral moment.
- Nostalgia/retro is a structurally durable niche because it targets older buyers (35-55) who are underserved by youth-oriented coloring books and have higher spending power.

### Gaps
- No confirmed Google Trends or Pinterest Trends data was retrievable due to network restrictions; Accio figures should be treated as proxies only.
- No data found on "cozy gaming" or "video game character" coloring books as a potential emerging niche — worth exploring separately.

## Key Question 5: What do KDP publishers and self-publishing communities say are the best current opportunities?

### Takeaway
Across multiple KDP-focused blogs, Substack newsletters, and YouTube-sourced recaps, the consensus is that "stacked" (multi-niche) books with specific audiences outperform single-topic generic books, with large-print-for-seniors and profession-specific coloring books named most often as reliably profitable, low-competition opportunities.

### Cited Findings
- One well-cited KDP analysis states: "A low-competition KDP niche in 2026 is a stacked coloring book, not a brand-new category" — combining two proven demand segments (e.g., "large print" + "mandala" + "seniors") — [bookcoverslab.com Low-Competition KDP Niches 2026](https://bookcoverslab.com/blog/low-competition-kdp-niches-2026)
- KDP community analysis says Amazon's coloring book catalog contains "well over 100,000 active titles" in 2026, meaning solo publishers must niche down aggressively — [KDP Easy Best Coloring Book Niches](https://www.kdpeasy.com/blog/best-coloring-book-niches-kdp)
- Average best-seller price point for coloring books on Amazon clusters around $7.99-$8.71 for broad titles, while specialized niche books average $17.99-$24.99 (attributed to Publishers Weekly data, unverified link) — [wiseppc.com Most Profitable KDP Niches](https://wiseppc.com/blog/most-profitable-amazon-kdp-niches/)
- KDP publishers cite faith-based coloring books as having a "stable royalty stream" and consistent demand — [recapio.com JeffSetLife "Basic" KDP Ideas](https://recapio.com/digest/don-t-sleep-on-these-basic-kdp-book-ideas-you-can-sell-on-amazon-by-jeffsetlife)
- AI illustration tools have lowered the barrier to entry, raising competition in broad categories while making niche-specific, high-quality interiors a stronger differentiator — [KDP Easy Coloring Book Trends 2026](https://www.kdpeasy.com/guides/coloring-book-trends-2026)
- KDP publishers use Publisher Rocket's category analyzer to find underserved Amazon subcategories and validate niche competition before creating books — [kindlepreneur.com How to Publish a Coloring Book](https://kindlepreneur.com/how-to-publish-a-coloring-book/) (page unreachable; cited in search)
- A color-by-number book for adults ranked 16,613 on Amazon is estimated to earn $600-$900/month (this figure is unverified and from a single seller's claim) — [lowcontentprofits.com KDP Niches 2026](https://lowcontentprofits.com/low-content-book-niches-kdp/)
- Large-print coloring books for seniors are described as among the "most durable pocket of demand, driven by care homes, occupational therapy and family gifting" — [inkfluenceai.com Coloring Book Niches 2026](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/coloring-book-niches-2026)
- Marketing through Pinterest and TikTok flip-through videos using narrow niches with covers that show the finished result is cited as the standard approach for KDP publishers in 2026 — [inkchamps.com 10 Best Niches 2026](https://inkchamps.com/blog/best-niches-for-coloring-books-amazon-kdp-2026/)

### Inferences
- The convergence on "stacking" is actionable: a publisher can target "Bold Easy Capybara Coloring Book for Adults — Stress Relief with Large Print Designs" and hit three demand segments simultaneously.
- The pricing data implies that generic low-price coloring books ($8) compete primarily on price, while niche books can command $17-$25 and still sell — meaning lower volume but higher margin per unit.
- AI-generated interiors are now pervasive; publishers who differentiate on illustration style (hand-drawn look, genuine grayscale photo processing, specific cultural aesthetics) will have a moat that AI-commodity books lack.

- No direct Reddit thread data found; searches returned only KDP blogs. The r/KDP and r/selfpublishing community sentiment on current 2026 niches could not be sampled directly.
- Publisher Rocket and Book Bolt current niche scores were not retrievable due to network restrictions; all competition estimates above are from third-party analyses, not direct tool exports.
- Could not access Amazon Best Sellers pages directly to confirm real-time BSR data; all ranking figures are from third-party tools and blog analyses.
