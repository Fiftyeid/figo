Now look beyond them. Beyond the last roofs, beyond the walls, to the west. There is another light out there. Not amber. Not warm. A duller glow, and a low haze, and the smell of smoke that does not come from any kitchen. That is the camp. That is where the army is. And that is where our story will go, in a little while. But not yet. For now, stay here, at this window. Let the city be a city for a few more minutes. Let the lamps be lamps, and the bell be a bell, and the dog in the street be a dog, sleeping in the exact centre of the road, in the way of everyone, and caring nothing for any of it. Because the truth is this: on the last night of a city, the most important things are never the walls. They are the small things that everyone does without thinking, and that nobody will do again. When you are ready, we will walk down from this tower, and we will go to the wall.
