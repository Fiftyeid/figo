Now let us cross the ditch. This is not an easy thing to ask of you. But we are only walking. Nobody will stop us. On this night, the ground between the two sides is the quietest place in the world. It is a strip of bare earth, and broken stone, and thin grass, and it belongs to no one. Halfway across, stop, and turn around, and look back at the city. It is beautiful. That is the strange and terrible thing. From here, with the mist around your feet and the hills beyond, the city looks like something in a book. The lamps along the top of the wall. The domes in the distance. The bell, for a moment, still ringing. You would not know, from here, that anything was wrong. Now turn back, and walk on into the camp.
