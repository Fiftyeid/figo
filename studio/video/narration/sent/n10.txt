Let us walk down the main street. Let us keep to the edge, where the shadows are, and let us not disturb anyone. Here is a tent with the flap pinned back. Inside, by a small lamp, a man is repairing a boot. He has a needle made of bone, and a length of waxed thread, and he is working slowly, pressing the needle through the leather with a small wooden clamp. His hands are careful. He is not thinking about the city. He is thinking about the boot. Here is another tent, and inside it, someone is singing, very quietly, in a language you do not know, a song with only four or five notes in it, going round and round. It is not a sad song. It is a song to work by, or to fall asleep to. It stops. There is a murmur. Then it starts again.
